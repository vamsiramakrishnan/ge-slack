Gemini Enterprise for Slack — UX mockup

Static mockup · spec: docs/EXPERIENCE.md

“Ask Gemini Enterprise about any conversation you choose, grounded on sources you pick, acting as you — and see, approve, and undo everything it posts.”  The ge-msft paradigm (seven verbs, verb × scope × ground, plan → approve → gate → ledger, one identity envelope) expressed through Slack’s own materials. Sample workspace: Acme, incident on the cache tier, invoker Alex Rivera (alex@acme.com).

Grammar cheat sheet/gemini <verb> [scope] [@grounds] [from:@person] ["instruction"] [--flags]

Verbs (intents)

askchatsummarizechatexplainchat rewritewritereviewannot.draftwritenotesannot.

Chat verbs answer (route send). Write/annotation verbs always produce a plan card (route runCommands) — the route is inferred from the verb, never toggled.

Scope — where

scope:thread#channelscope:messagepermalinkscope:canvas(F07…)dmsearch("…")from:@maya

Default: the thread if invoked in one, else a 24h channel window. You must be a member of every conversation in scope.

@ grounds — what it’s grounded on

@unit@policies@jira@drive@this@web

<@U…> mentions are people (filters, assignees); literal @alias tokens are grounds.

Flags

--since 7d--public--to #ch--as me|service--dry-run--tone brief

Durations: h d w. --public posts in channel instead of “only visible to you”.

Control verbs — never call the model: help connect disconnect whoami as sources automate automations undo  ·  Eight doors, one typed Invocation { verb, scope, grounds, people, instruction, flags, origin } → one dispatcher.
